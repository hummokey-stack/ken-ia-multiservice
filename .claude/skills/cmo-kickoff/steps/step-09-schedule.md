# Step 9: Launch Schedule

## Context Loading

Load from `{output_folder}/` (if exists):

- `tasks.md` — the tasks to schedule

## Your Task

Create a day-by-day launch schedule that maps tasks to specific days across 1-2 weeks. The schedule should be realistic for a solo founder and account for optimal posting times per channel.

## Instructions

### 1. Determine Schedule Type

**If pre-launch:** Create a launch countdown schedule (Week -1 prep, Week 0 launch)
**If just-launched:** Create a post-launch momentum schedule (this week + next week)
**If growing:** Create a growth sprint schedule (2-week campaign)

### 2. Build Pre-Launch Checklist

Things that must be ready before marketing starts:
- [ ] Landing page / website is live and working
- [ ] Product is accessible (can people sign up / download / try it?)
- [ ] One-line pitch is clear (from positioning)
- [ ] At least one visual asset (screenshot, demo GIF, or video)
- [ ] Email capture is set up (if applicable)
- [ ] Social accounts are created on recommended channels

### 3. Map Tasks to Days

Create a day-by-day schedule:

```
## Week 1

| Day | Channel | Task | Time Needed |
|-----|---------|------|-------------|
| Mon | [channel] | [specific task from tasks.md] | [time] |
| Tue | [channel] | [specific task] | [time] |
| ... |

## Week 2

| Day | Channel | Task | Time Needed |
| ... |
```

**Scheduling rules:**
- Don't overload any single day — max 1-2 hours of marketing per day
- Space channel posts to avoid looking spammy
- Account for optimal posting times from channel guides
- Put highest-impact tasks on Tuesday-Thursday (highest engagement days for most platforms)
- Leave weekends light (monitoring and responding only)
- If HN launch: block the entire day for comment engagement

### 4. Add Post-Schedule Guidance

After the 2-week schedule:
- What to do after the schedule is complete
- How to know what's working (metrics to watch per channel)
- When to double down vs. pivot
- Suggest running `/cmo-help` to get next recommendations

### 5. Save Launch Plan

Copy template from `../templates/launch-plan.template.md` to `{output_folder}/launch-plan.md`.
Populate with checklist + schedule. Update frontmatter.
