# Step 5: Positioning & Messaging

## Context Loading

Load from `{output_folder}/` (if exists):
- `product-profile.md` — product details, audience, stage, platform presence, budget
- `personas.md` — who we're talking to
- `voice-profile.md` — how we sound

## Your Task

Craft clear product positioning and core messaging that will drive all strategy and content. Positioning answers: "Why should THIS specific audience choose THIS product over alternatives?" Messaging translates that into words they'll actually use.

## Instructions

### 1. Review Available Context

Read all loaded artifacts. Pay special attention to:
- Product differentiator from product-profile.md
- Persona pain points from personas.md
- Product stage (pre-launch vs. growing changes positioning)
- Current platform presence (zero audience = different messaging than established)

If key artifacts are missing, ask for minimum context inline.

### 2. Draft Positioning Statement

Create a positioning statement using this framework:

"For [specific audience from personas],
who [specific pain point / current frustration],
[product name] is the [category]
that [key differentiator / what makes it special].
Unlike [main alternative they currently use],
[product name] [specific advantage]."

**Example:**
"For freelance web developers who waste 30 minutes every morning figuring out what to work on, TaskFlow is the CLI task manager that respects how developers actually work. Unlike Jira, TaskFlow lives in your terminal and takes 30 seconds to plan your day."

Present to founder: "Here's how I'd position your product. Does this feel right? What would you change?"

### 3. Define Core Messages

Based on confirmed positioning, create 3-5 core messages:

Each message should:
- Address a specific persona pain point
- Explain WHY your product solves it (not just WHAT it does)
- Be usable as a tweet, headline, or conversation starter
- Sound like the founder's voice (reference voice profile)

**Format:**
```
Message 1: [Pain point] → [Your solution] → [Outcome]
"[Actual message in founder's voice]"
Best for: [which channel/context this works in]
```

### 4. Adapt for Product Stage

**If pre-launch:** Messages focus on the problem and vision. "I'm building X because Y is broken."
**If just-launched:** Messages focus on early results and proof. "Here's what happened when I shipped X."
**If growing:** Messages focus on social proof and differentiation. "X users switched from Y because Z."

### 5. Validate with Founder

Present positioning + messages. Ask:
- "Does this sound like something you'd actually say?"
- "Would your target users understand this in 5 seconds?"
- "Is there anything that feels off or forced?"

Refine until confirmed.
