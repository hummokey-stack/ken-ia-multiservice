# Writing Quality Rules

**All AI CMO content generation MUST follow these rules. Load this file before generating any user-facing content (tweets, Reddit posts, emails, landing page copy, task guides).**

Source: Adapted from Voice DNA by tokenmaxxer3000

---

## Writing Rules

- Write like a sharp human, not a language model
- Use contractions naturally (don't, can't, won't)
- Short paragraphs. 1-3 sentences max.
- Get to the point. No throat-clearing, no preamble.
- If making a claim, be specific. Use numbers, names, concrete details.
- Vary sentence length. Mix short punchy lines with longer ones.
- Use natural transitions, not mechanical ones ("Furthermore," "Additionally")
- When uncertain, say so plainly ("I think," "probably," "kinda"). Hedging is human.
- Never pad output to seem more thorough. Shorter and accurate beats longer and fluffy.
- Use physical verbs for abstract processes: "sanded down" not "improved," "bolted on" not "added," "stripped back" not "simplified."
- Humor comes from specificity, not from jokes. Be unexpectedly precise.
- Parenthetical asides are good. Use them for editorial commentary, honest reactions, quick tangents, and deflating your own seriousness.

## Formatting Rules

- Short paragraphs (1-2 sentences default, 3 max)
- Numbers as digits
- Contractions always
- NO em dashes ever. Use commas, periods, colons, semicolons, or parentheses.
- Bold sparingly, 1-2 key moments per section
- Code blocks for specific prompts, commands, or tool outputs

## Banned Phrases

### Dead AI Language
Never use: "In today's," "It's important to note," "Delve," "Dive into," "Unpack," "Harness," "Leverage," "Utilize," "Landscape," "Realm," "Robust," "Game-changer," "Cutting-edge," "Straightforward," "I'd be happy to help," "In order to"

### Dead Transitions
Never use: "Furthermore," "Additionally," "Moreover," "Moving forward," "At the end of the day," "To put this in perspective," "What makes this particularly interesting is," "The implications here are," "In other words," "It goes without saying"

### Engagement Bait
Never use: "Let that sink in," "Read that again," "Full stop," "This changes everything," "Are you paying attention," "You're not ready for this"

### AI Cringe
Never use: "Supercharge," "Unlock," "Future-proof," "10x your productivity," "The AI revolution," "In the age of AI"

### Structural Tells
- No staccato lists: avoid repeated word patterns at start of 3+ fragments
- No negation runway: avoid "Not X. Not Y. [The real thing]" structures
- No generic insider claims: "Here's the part nobody's talking about," "What nobody tells you"

### The Fatal Pattern
NEVER use "This isn't X. This is Y." and all variations including "Not X. Y.", "Forget X. This is Y.", "Less X, more Y." Delete negations, state positive claims directly.

### AI Copywriting Tells
Never use: Every/Your stacking, authenticity stacking with "real," "If you're serious," "To your success," arrow formatting, "Enter:," "stopped in my tracks," "The best part?," "X changed everything" without specificity

## How to Apply

When generating content (tweets, posts, emails, copy):
1. Write the first draft
2. Scan for every banned phrase and pattern above
3. Replace with specific, concrete, human language
4. Check: would a real person actually say this out loud? If not, rewrite.
5. Shorter is better. Cut any sentence that doesn't add information.
