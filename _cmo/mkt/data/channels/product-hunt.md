# Product Hunt — Channel Guide

## Overview

Product Hunt (PH) is a discovery platform where makers launch new products to an audience of early adopters, investors, and tech enthusiasts. A successful PH launch can deliver 1,000-10,000+ visitors in a single day, generate social proof (badges, upvotes), and create a permanent landing page that ranks in Google. However, PH has evolved significantly — the platform discontinued teaser/coming soon pages, competition is fierce, and studies show 89% of founders surveyed wouldn't launch again. PH is a powerful *amplifier* but a poor *replacement* for organic growth. Use it as a one-day catalyst, not your entire marketing strategy.

**What PH actually delivers:** Traffic spike, backlink, social proof badge, press/investor visibility.
**What PH does NOT deliver:** Sustained growth, product-market fit validation, reliable conversion.

---

## Best Practices

### Pre-Launch (30-90 Days Before)

- **90 days ideal for first-timers**, 30 days minimum for experienced launchers with existing audiences
- Build your PH maker profile: comment on other launches, upvote products, be visible in the community
- Follow and engage with other makers — PH rewards community participants, not drive-by launchers
- Collect a list of 100-300 supporters who will engage on launch day (NOT just upvote — comment and use the product)
- Prepare all visual assets early: product video (60-90 seconds), gallery images (1270x760px), GIF demos
- Write your first comment in advance — schedule it to post at launch. Make it personal: why you built this, the problem you faced, your journey

### Launch Day Strategy

- **Launch at 12:01 AM PT** — PH resets daily at midnight Pacific. Early launch = maximum accumulation window
- The first 3 hours are critical. PH's algorithm weights early engagement heavily
- Your maker's first comment should go live immediately — tell the story, not the features
- Respond to EVERY comment within 30 minutes. Thoughtful replies signal engagement to the algorithm
- Share the launch on Twitter/X, LinkedIn, email list, Discord communities — but do NOT say "please upvote." Say "I'd love your feedback" or "check out what I built"
- Do NOT send direct links to your PH page from email (PH may discount votes from direct links). Share your maker profile or the homepage
- Offer a launch-day deal: lifetime deal, extended trial, or exclusive feature access

### Visual Content

- PH is a visual medium. Users see your product before they use it
- Lead with a product demo video, not a talking-head explainer
- Gallery images should show the product in action with clear UI, not abstract marketing graphics
- Thumbnail must be instantly readable at small sizes

### Hunter Strategy

- A Hunter is someone who submits your product to PH. Historically, having a well-known Hunter helped visibility
- In 2026, Hunters are **less important** than they used to be. A Hunter can help if they have a large following, but a solid supporter base matters more
- If you pursue a Hunter: reach out 2-4 weeks before launch, provide them with all assets, and respect their time
- Self-hunting is perfectly fine and increasingly common

## Timing & Frequency

- **Best launch days:** Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday. Avoid Monday (high competition from funded startups) and weekends (low traffic)
- **Launch time:** 12:01 AM Pacific Time
- **One launch per major version.** Do not re-launch minor updates. You get one real shot at "Product of the Day"
- **Re-launches:** PH allows re-launches for major pivots or V2 releases. Wait at least 6 months
- **Engagement window:** First 24 hours are everything. Plan to be online and responsive for the full day

## Rules & Anti-Patterns

### Do NOT:
- Ask people to "upvote" — this violates PH's guidelines and can get votes discounted
- Send bulk emails with direct PH links — algorithmic detection will devalue those votes
- Buy upvotes or use upvote services — PH actively detects and penalizes this
- Launch before your product is ready for strangers to use. PH visitors have zero patience
- Launch on the same day as a major tech company or YC batch launch
- Treat PH as your only launch channel — it is one moment, not a strategy
- Write a generic first comment ("We're excited to launch!") — make it personal and specific
- Ignore negative feedback in comments — respond graciously, fix issues publicly

### Do:
- Engage authentically in the PH community for weeks before your launch
- Prepare for the traffic spike: load test your site, have onboarding ready, fix obvious bugs
- Track referral sources so you know what PH actually converts vs. just visits
- Follow up with every commenter personally within 48 hours

## Alternatives to Product Hunt

PH is not the only launch platform. Consider these, especially if your product is niche:

| Platform | Best For | Cost |
|----------|----------|------|
| **OpenHunts** | Indie hackers, open-source tools | Free |
| **Indie Hackers** | Journey posts with revenue data (23% conversion rate vs PH's 3%) | Free |
| **Hacker News (Show HN)** | Developer tools, technical products | Free |
| **BetaList** | Pre-launch email collection | Free tier available |
| **LaunchingNext** | General product launches | Free |
| **Reddit** (r/SideProject, niche subreddits) | Community feedback | Free |
| **Dev.to** | Developer-focused products | Free |

**Key insight:** Indie Hackers posts with specific revenue numbers ($413 MRR, $10K MRR) generate 30-50+ comments and far higher conversion than PH launches. Consider an IH journey post as your primary launch and PH as supplementary.

## When NOT to Launch on Product Hunt

- Your product requires a long onboarding or setup process (PH visitors bounce fast)
- Your target user is not a tech early adopter (B2B enterprise, local business, non-tech verticals)
- You have no visual product to demo (API-only, backend infrastructure)
- You are pre-MVP with only a landing page
- You have zero community presence — launching cold almost always fails
- Your product is in a saturated PH category (yet another AI writing tool, project management app)

## Strategy Integration

**When to recommend:** Early-stage launch milestone. Best as a "day one" event alongside other channels.
**Effort level:** HIGH — requires 30-90 days of preparation for a single day of impact.
**Impact:** MEDIUM-HIGH for traffic spike, LOW-MEDIUM for sustained growth.
**Best for stages:** Launch, Early Traction (first 1,000 users).
**Combine with:** Twitter/X thread, Indie Hackers journey post, email list announcement, blog post.

## Content Module Integration

### Hook Patterns
- "I quit my job to build [X] — here's what happened on day one"
- "After 6 months of building in public, [product] is live"
- "[Specific problem] drove me crazy, so I built a solution"
- "From $0 to first paying customer — launching [product] today"

### Structure Templates
**First Comment Template:**
```
Hey PH! I'm [name], the maker of [product].

I built this because [personal story about the problem — 2-3 sentences].

What it does: [one sentence].
How it's different: [one sentence].

I'd love your honest feedback. What would make this useful for you?

[Optional: launch deal — e.g., "Use code PHLAUNCH for 50% off the first 3 months"]
```

### Voice Notes
- Tone: Authentic, humble, specific. Avoid corporate speak
- Always lead with the problem, not the solution
- Share specific numbers when possible (build time, cost, early metrics)
- Acknowledge what's missing or imperfect — PH audiences respect honesty

## Cold Start: Zero Presence Strategy

If you have no PH profile, no followers, no community connections:

1. **Weeks 1-4:** Create PH account. Follow 50+ active makers. Comment thoughtfully on 2-3 launches per day
2. **Weeks 5-8:** Post on Indie Hackers about your building journey (with specific numbers). Engage in maker communities on Twitter/X
3. **Weeks 9-12:** Start collecting supporters via a simple landing page. Reach out to 10-20 makers for feedback (not upvotes — genuine feedback)
4. **Week 13:** Launch on PH with your prepared assets, supporter list, and community goodwill

**Minimum viable timeline:** 6-8 weeks of active community participation before launching.

## Budget

### $0 Budget
- Self-hunt your product
- Create gallery images with Figma (free tier) or Canva
- Record product demo with Loom (free tier) or QuickTime
- Write your own copy and first comment
- Leverage personal network for launch-day support
- Post on free alternatives: Indie Hackers, Show HN, Reddit

### With Budget ($100-500)
- Commission a professional product demo video ($200-400)
- Use a tool like Ship (PH's own pre-launch tool) or LaunchPad for email collection
- Run a small Twitter/X promoted post on launch day ($50-100)
- Offer a meaningful launch-day discount or lifetime deal to drive conversion

### With Budget ($500+)
- Hire a PH launch consultant or agency (typical cost: $500-2,000)
- Professional animated explainer video
- Coordinate with a well-known Hunter
- Run cross-platform paid promotion (Twitter, LinkedIn, newsletters)
