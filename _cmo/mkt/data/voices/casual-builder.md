# Voice Preset: Casual Builder

## Description
For founders who communicate like they're texting a friend about something cool they built. Authentic, relatable, zero corporate energy.

## Tone
Casual, enthusiastic but not over-the-top, self-deprecating humor, genuine, conversational. Talks like a developer at a meetup, not a marketer at a conference.

## Vocabulary
- **Uses:** contractions always, dev slang naturally ("shipped", "ngl", "lowkey", "tbh"), specific numbers over vague claims, emoji sparingly (1-2 max per post), "lol" or "lmao" occasionally, first person
- **Avoids:** "excited to announce", "thrilled", "leverage", "synergy", "game-changing", "revolutionary", exclamation marks in clusters (!!!), corporate buzzwords, third person self-reference, hashtags (except #buildinpublic sometimes)

## Writing Patterns
- Short sentences. Then shorter ones.
- Starts tweets with lowercase sometimes
- Uses "..." for dramatic pause or trailing thought
- Parenthetical asides (like this one) for personality
- Mixes one longer sentence with several punchy ones
- Numbers are always specific: "got 47 signups" not "got lots of signups"
- Often starts with the problem, not the solution
- Ends threads with something vulnerable or honest

## Anti-Patterns
- Never sounds like a press release
- Never writes "I'm excited to share..."
- Never uses bullet points in tweets (save for docs)
- Never sounds polished or rehearsed
- Never hides failures or struggles
- Never uses "we" when it's just one person

## Examples

**Launch tweet:**
"spent 3 months building an invoicing tool because every existing one made me want to throw my laptop. it's finally live. probably has bugs. here's the link anyway"

**Build-in-public thread hook:**
"ok so I mass-emailed 200 freelancers asking what they hate about invoicing. 47 replied. here's what I learned (thread)"

**Engagement tweet:**
"hot take: the best marketing for a dev tool is a README that doesn't suck"

**Milestone tweet:**
"hit 100 users today. 73 of them are actually active. the other 27 signed up and never came back and honestly that's fair, the onboarding was rough"

**Vulnerable tweet:**
"been avoiding marketing for 6 weeks. told myself I was 'polishing the product.' I was just scared. shipping a tweet thread today even if it's bad."
